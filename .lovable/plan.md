# Refresh daily-writer keyword research

## Goal
Bring the daily writer into line with the current 151-tool catalogue and the capabilities recently corrected from the Marketplace, using current US Semrush data rather than assumptions.

## Confirmed gaps
- The catalogue has 151 tools, but the keyword file contains only 140 records.
- These 11 tools have no researched keyword record and currently receive only a generic fallback prompt: UGC Factory, Viral Clips, AI Video Enhancer, AI Council Mode, AI Social Media Publisher, Content Manager, AI ReWriter, Brand Voice, AI Editor, AI Image Assistant, and AI Realtime Image.
- Existing agent and automation records need revalidation because their capabilities have changed, especially Blogger Agent, Social Media Agent, Marketing Bot, DM & Comment Agent, Deep Research, Product Photoshoot, Fashion Studio, and Creative Suite.
- The DM & Comment topic contains two obsolete internal paths (`/smart-inbox` and `/social-media-agent`).
- New tools automatically enter informational rotation, but major commercial and use-case opportunities need explicit roadmap topics.

## Implementation
1. **Research the uncovered and updated tools**
   - Run current US Semrush research for each relevant tool using capability-accurate seed phrases.
   - Select keywords by intent and feature fit, not raw volume alone.
   - Treat missing Semrush data as unknown, never as zero, and avoid unrelated broad terms with misleading intent.

2. **Complete and correct the keyword database**
   - Add records for all 11 uncovered tools with primary keyword, volume, difficulty, related terms, and genuine question queries.
   - Refresh stale records for materially updated agents, automation, video, image, and writing tools where live data or Marketplace-aligned wording differs.
   - Keep every keyword mapped to the exact public tool slug and remove no valid existing coverage.

3. **Expand the editorial queue selectively**
   - Add high-value topics for new capabilities, prioritising AI video enhancement, AI UGC, blog automation, social publishing/automation, live image generation, and brand voice.
   - Preserve the existing 30/30/25/15 rotation and avoid duplicating published or already queued article intent.
   - Link each topic to the exact relevant tool pages and established cluster articles.

4. **Fix internal-link and cluster alignment**
   - Replace obsolete internal paths with the current tool URLs.
   - Map AI Agents, AI Automation, AI Social Media, AI Chat, and AI Productivity to appropriate sibling clusters instead of the generic content fallback.
   - Add Blogger Agent and the new marketplace tools where they are genuinely relevant to existing commercial topics.

5. **Update the strategy record and verify**
   - Record the September 2026 refresh, sources, new topic priorities, and unknown-data handling in the blog strategy document.
   - Verify all 151 public tool slugs have keyword coverage, every queued link resolves, IDs are unique, content-mix rotation remains unchanged, and the site builds cleanly.
   - Tutorials will not be changed.

## Initial Semrush signals
Current US data already confirms strong opportunities including AI Video Enhancer (~4,400 searches/month, difficulty 50), AI ReWriter (~49,500, difficulty 78), AI UGC Generator (~140, difficulty 46), AI blog automation (~70, difficulty 21), Brand Voice Generator (~40, difficulty 25), and AI Realtime Image Generator (~10, difficulty 0). These are estimates and will be checked against intent before being stored.
