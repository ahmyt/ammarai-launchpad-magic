# Premium obsidian homepage redesign

## Direction
Redesign the homepage using the selected **Premium obsidian** direction, adapted to AmmarAI rather than copied literally:

- Deep charcoal and near-black surfaces with refined warm layers
- AmmarAI’s richer orange as the signature accent; no indigo/purple-led palette
- Distinctive editorial display typography paired with a highly legible sans serif
- Precise borders, restrained glow, subtle texture, and disciplined shadows
- Quiet, premium motion with reduced-motion support

The existing logo, messaging, navigation, section order, tool names, links, videos, reviews, search, and CMS-driven content remain intact.

## Homepage work

1. **Global visual system**
   - Introduce homepage-compatible obsidian semantic tokens, surface levels, refined orange states, shadows, and texture.
   - Upgrade the display font and type scale while preserving readability across mobile and desktop.
   - Refine shared buttons, cards, headings, and section spacing so the page feels cohesive rather than like repeated grids.

2. **Navigation and opening section**
   - Restyle the sticky navigation as a restrained translucent obsidian bar with clearer hierarchy.
   - Recompose the opening viewport around the existing headline, supporting copy, CTAs, and functional “Find the right tool” search.
   - Give the search panel a premium console treatment with better suggestion states, focus behavior, and depth.
   - Keep the first viewport focused on the AmmarAI product rather than generic AI artwork.

3. **Trust and product proof**
   - Upgrade the approved-logo marquee for the dark surface while retaining proper alt text, pause behavior, and reduced-motion fallback.
   - Present flagship tools with stronger hierarchy and varied composition instead of eight visually identical boxes.
   - Elevate the existing playable video library using the real local samples, cinematic framing, and clearer tool attribution.

4. **Library, features, and comparison**
   - Restyle category discovery, popular/recent tools, workspace features, and use cases with a more deliberate editorial rhythm.
   - Turn the value comparison into a high-contrast premium decision section while retaining all CMS-managed copy and CTAs.
   - Preserve all current destinations and interactions.

5. **Reviews, articles, FAQ, and conversion close**
   - Integrate the genuine review widget into the obsidian system without changing submission, moderation, filtering, or browsing behavior.
   - Preserve review paragraph formatting and responsive layouts.
   - Refine blog cards, FAQ rows, final CTA, and footer into a confident closing sequence.

6. **Responsive polish and validation**
   - Tune the complete page at mobile and desktop sizes, ensuring text, controls, videos, dialogs, and cards never overlap or compress badly.
   - Verify search suggestions, menus, CTAs, videos, review dialogs, review filters, and review submission.
   - Confirm keyboard focus, contrast, semantic headings, reduced motion, and production build health.

## Technical scope

- Primary files: homepage route, global design tokens/styles, shared site primitives, header, footer, buttons, tool cards, trust strip, and customer reviews.
- Existing backend, CMS data, routes, review records, and business logic remain unchanged.
- New visual values will be semantic tokens in the global design system rather than hardcoded page colors.
- The redesign will not add fabricated testimonials, generic stock imagery, or unrelated features.
