# Magic AI feature audit: what AmmarAI is missing

I read all seven pages of Magic AI's release site plus the tool documentation. The site today
has 66 pages, and almost all of them describe single-shot generators: write this, rewrite that,
make an image. Magic AI's current platform is a different product — agents that work on their
own, a CRM, phone calls, publishing, and full creative and video editing suites.

Below is every capability worth a page, with the substance behind it so the copy can be
concrete rather than generic.

## Group 1 — Autonomous agents (nothing like this on the site today)

1. **AI Agent Builder** — build agents conversationally or on a drag-and-drop canvas. Agents
   run on a schedule or when a message arrives, talk over Telegram, Slack and WhatsApp, and
   connect to Gmail, Outlook, calendars and productivity tools. Examples: a daily-report
   assistant, an email agent that reads/summarises/drafts, a business monitor that reports to Slack.
2. **Phone Call Agents** — answer and place real calls with human-like speech: appointment
   scheduling, bookings, reservations, support, follow-ups, confirmations, lead qualification,
   24/7 availability.
3. **Social Media Agent** — autonomous social manager. Plans the monthly calendar, writes
   on-brand posts at scale across multiple platforms and accounts, reads performance and
   audience behaviour, and adapts posting times, formats and creative direction.
4. **DM & Comment Agent** — visual automation builder for auto-replies to comments and DMs,
   lead capture and campaign promotion; now also covers YouTube comments.
5. **Blogger Agent** — bulk blog posts published or scheduled straight to WordPress from your
   topics, keywords, tone and frequency, with SEO handled automatically.
6. **AI Deep Research** — multi-source research runs with live web search.

## Group 2 — Sales and customer operations

7. **AI-Powered CRM** — clients, companies, deals, pipelines, sales tracking, invoices,
   payments, tasks, reports. The AI CRM Assistant answers questions about your data, creates
   and updates records, analyses sales and builds presentations from CRM data. Reachable from
   WhatsApp and Telegram.
8. **Smart Inbox** — one place for every conversation: ticket statuses, archiving, in-chat
   search, contact tagging and segmentation, internal notes, response-time analytics, CRM sync.
9. **Marketing Bot** — WhatsApp/Meta campaigns over the official API, bulk audience import,
   delivery and engagement analytics, per-plan sending limits.

## Group 3 — External chatbot (current page is badly out of date)

10. **External Chatbot v3** — branded chatbot homepage, searchable help center, lead-capture
    forms that sync to the CRM, file attachments, emoji, GDPR consent, chat export, image
    analysis, feedback collection.
11. **Sales Agent** — WooCommerce and Shopify product recommendations, order tracking, variant
    and image display, shopping support.
12. **Booking Agent** — Calendly and Cal.com scheduling inside the chat.
13. **Voice Chat** — visitors switch between talking and typing to the assistant.
14. **Channels** — Instagram, WhatsApp, Telegram and more feeding one unified inbox.

## Group 4 — Creative and visual production

15. **AI Image Pro** — ratio, style and variation control, trainable custom styles, public
    gallery and creator profiles, media library with real-time history, smart workflows for
    product shots, thumbnails, avatars and placements; can run as a public front end.
16. **AI Image Editing** — cleanup, inpainting, reimagine, generative fill, upscaler, sketch to
    image, background remove and replace.
17. **Creative Suite + AI Designer** — describe a social post, ad, banner or promo and get an
    editable design; one-click background removal, gradients, on-brand asset kits, annotation.
18. **AI Photoshoot** — studio-quality product photography from prompts, templates or
    background replacement: studio, lifestyle, UGC-style and billboard visuals.
19. **AI Fashion Photoshoot / Virtual Try-On** — products on realistic AI models with
    consistent lighting and poses; swap models, edit scenes, and turn stills into product video.

## Group 5 — Video and audio production

20. **AI Video Pro** — frontier video models (Seedance, Kling, Veo, Sora-class) with background
    removal and fine-grained controls.
21. **AI Video Editor** — trim, split, timeline editing, text and media overlays, combining
    clips, and mixing AI video, music and voiceover in one place.
22. **AI Captions** — subtitle any clip with styled caption templates in seconds.
23. **AI Dubbing** — translate YouTube, TikTok or uploaded video into dozens of languages at
    once, with automatic language and speaker detection.
24. **UGC Creator & UGC Factory** — upload product and avatar images, script dialogue,
    voiceover and character action, pick camera settings; choose from stock actors, design an
    AI actor or make a digital twin of yourself.
25. **YouTube & Shorts publishing** — generate or upload video and publish straight to YouTube,
    with automatic handling of Shorts aspect ratio and duration.
26. **AI Music Pro** — full tracks, melodies and instrumentals by genre, mood and length
    (replaces the current thin music page).

## Group 6 — The assistant itself

27. **AI Chat Pro** — connectors to Notion, Gmail, Outlook, Google Drive and Google Calendar;
    smart memory and behaviour rules; folders; chat navigator; smart highlight; temporary chat;
    follow-up question suggestions; multi-model answers and Council Mode where several models
    answer together; real-time web search on every model.
28. **AI Persona & Skills** — reusable personas with live previews, plus skills that extend
    what the assistant can do.
29. **Command Search** — one palette to find tools, templates, documents, settings and
    conversations, run actions, and start a chat.

## Group 7 — Platform and commercial pages

30. **Models page** — Sora 2 Pro, Veo 3.1, GPT-5 Pro, Grok 4, Claude Opus/Sonnet, Gemini 3.1
    Pro, Kling 2.6/3, ElevenLabs v3, Nano Banana 2 — one page that sells model choice.
31. **Pricing page update** — shared credits pool (one balance across every tool, with cost
    shown before each generation) and discounts.

## Suggested build order

- **Phase 1 (biggest gap):** AI Agent Builder, Phone Call Agents, AI CRM, Social Media Agent,
  Blogger Agent — plus two new directory categories, "AI Agents" and "AI Sales & CRM".
- **Phase 2:** External chatbot rewrite with its sales, booking, voice and channel sub-pages,
  Smart Inbox, Marketing Bot.
- **Phase 3:** Image Pro, image editing, Creative Suite, Photoshoot, Fashion/Try-On.
- **Phase 4:** Video Pro, Video Editor, Captions, Dubbing, UGC, YouTube publishing, Music Pro.
- **Phase 5:** Chat Pro, personas and skills, command search, models page, pricing update.
- **Phase 6:** the ~95 missing Custom Templates in Group 8, built tab by tab.

## Technical notes

Every new tool is a data entry in `src/data/tools-*.ts` following the existing `Tool` shape, so
each automatically gets its `/{slug}` page, FAQ schema, breadcrumbs, related links and a
directory listing. Two new values join `ToolCategory` in `src/data/types.ts` and
`categoryOrder` in `src/data/tools.ts`, with matching entries in the discovery search intent
map. Homepage featured tools, the comparison section and use-case toolkits get refreshed as
pages land. No database or CMS schema changes are needed.
