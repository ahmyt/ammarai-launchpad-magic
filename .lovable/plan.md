# High-quality video samples for every video tool

## Goal
Replace every current video-tool sample with a cohesive, premium video library inspired by the four supplied references: photoreal commercial imagery, a clear subject, polished lighting and colour, smooth cinematic motion, and strong mobile presentation.

## Scope
Create **20 output videos across all 13 video tools**:
- AI Video Pro: 4
- AI Avatar Video Generator: 2
- AI Image to Video: 2
- AI URL to Video & Influencer: 3
- AI Video Editor, AI Captions, AI Dubbing, AI UGC Creator, AI YouTube & Shorts Publisher, Viral Clips, AI Video to Video, UGC Factory, and AI Video Enhancer: 1 each

Tutorials will not be edited.

## Production approach
- Use the supplied videos as visual references, not as public sample content.
- Give each sample a distinct, capability-accurate concept rather than repeating the same product shot.
- Target premium vertical delivery for social-first examples, with 720p or better output, cinematic 24 fps motion, approximately 8–10 seconds, clean English text where text is required, and usable audio where the workflow requires speech or sound.
- Keep source and result logically matched for Video Editor, Captions, Dubbing, YouTube Publisher, Video to Video, and Video Enhancer.
- Preserve the two existing source images for Image to Video and animate those exact images.
- Build Captions, enhancement, reframing, and other deterministic transformations from matching source footage so the visible result proves the named capability.
- Show UGC Factory as a polished batch-style creator output, clearly distinct from the single-video UGC Creator example.
- Generate sequentially to respect video-generation limits, review each result, and do not silently retry blocked or failed generations.

## Site integration
- Store the approved videos through the project asset flow and keep self-hosted media copies where the existing Plesk setup requires them.
- Add the missing UGC Factory and AI Video Enhancer samples.
- Replace all existing output mappings and update captions only where needed to describe the new footage accurately.
- Regenerate poster frames so every sample has a sharp, representative first view and never appears blank.
- Keep the current tool-page design, controls, routes, and written capabilities unchanged.

## Quality checks
- Review every generated clip for subject consistency, motion quality, anatomy, product integrity, readable English text, audio, and visual artifacts.
- Verify each source/result pair shows the exact claimed change and does not imply unsupported capabilities.
- Check all 13 tool pages on desktop and mobile for playback, posters, sizing, controls, loading, and overflow.
- Confirm all 20 outputs load successfully, links remain valid, there are no console or build errors, and tutorial changes remain exactly 0.
